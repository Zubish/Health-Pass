# Clinical treatment and session controls

Confirmed by the owner on 13 October 2026. This extends care operations without changing workspace identities, sharing consent, role-denial precedence or immutable source records.

## Clinical decisions and treatment

A reviewed physician with admissions.decide records their own admission or discharge decision, reason, observations, actual decision time and recorded time against an active ward/observation episode. There is one immutable decision of each kind per episode. Admission requires confirming physician responsibility; discharge requires a prior admission decision. Administrative episode creation remains a separate action. Once clinically admitted, an episode cannot be ended operationally until its clinical discharge decision is recorded. Historical and erroneously opened operational episodes are not retrospectively labelled as physician decisions.

Prescriptions are signed structured orders authored by a reviewed general medical professional granted prescriptions.create. They contain medicine/strength/formulation, dose, route, frequency, indication, instructions, start/end times and prescribing identity/time. Inpatient orders are linked to an active episode; outpatient orders require a local registered branch. Prescription changes require stopping with a reason and a signed replacement. Signed content cannot be rewritten or deleted. This is prescribing and recording, not automated dose advice, interaction checking, drug catalogue integration or dispensing.

Nurses explicitly granted medication.administer record inpatient given/omitted/refused/held outcomes against scheduled dose slots, or as-needed prescriptions. Recorded dose, actual/due/recorded time, author, observations and reason are retained. Reasons are mandatory for non-given doses, PRN or a changed administered dose. Only valid due slots within the course may be used; duplicates, future outcomes, stopped orders, ended episodes and unassigned professionals are blocked. Ending an episode closes its ward medication orders; it does not issue outpatient continuation prescriptions.

Ward observations are independent of visits and include bounded structured vitals, free-text observations, measured/recorded times, author and discipline. The patient chart displays the longitudinal records and a recorded-value trend without interpreting results. Visit vitals gain free-text comments too. Clinical category sharing remains enforced: medication records need medication read access, prescriptions additionally need general contribution authority, administrations need nursing contribution authority, and ward observations require their reviewed discipline. Patient accounts can read their own multi-facility records but cannot author professional entries. Administrators do not gain clinical access.

Existing role customisations are preserved. New role templates expose reviewed physician treatment permissions and nurse observation/administration permissions; administrators review new keys for existing roles. A new permission cannot override a user's reviewed scope, department restriction or patient sharing grant.

## Session management

The mandatory 30-minute inactivity expiry and 30-day absolute session expiry are server enforced for every account and cannot be changed by users or administrators. An optional eight-hour absolute session limit is initially enabled, preserving existing protection, and can be toggled by any user in Session management. Changing it affects that user's existing sessions as well. HealthPass does not silently refresh the fixed 30-day deadline, and does not store passwords or email in the workspace preference. The owner accepted the current “Remember this workspace on this device” wording and its thirty-day explanation. Browser password-manager autofill remains independent of fresh server authentication.

Session settings list active sessions and allow the account holder to revoke them. Successful sign-ins record a random HttpOnly device identifier, generic browser/platform label, country if supplied by the trusted Vercel hop, hashed IP context, time and informational new-device/country-change flags. Known-identity failed password attempts are recorded privately; no account lookup is disclosed in error responses. Durable attempt limits supplement existing process limits. Device and location recognition is not identity proof; VPNs, travel and browser changes can produce alerts. Locations are approximate or unavailable. No external alert emails are sent. The app surfaces unreviewed activity and permits reviewing it; optional MFA remains supported. Raw IP addresses and raw user-agent strings are not stored.

## Action timestamps and history

Clinical decisions, prescriptions, administrations and ward observations have dedicated author and action/recording times. Episode/team workflow history exposes actor names with timestamps. New care-area and team creation timestamps are stored without inventing dates for historical rows. Every successful authenticated mutation additionally appends a timestamped own-account action entry accessible outside audit in Session management. This contains action metadata, not clinical text. Signed and treatment history remains append-only. Clinical list views are currently bounded; larger deployments need pagination rather than assuming full history is loaded.
